# Product Requirements Document: PersonalSavings (PS)

## Product Summary

PS is a  web application that helps individuals to observe, transform and save their bank statments, spendings, income and saving goals accross mulutiple bank accounts.

## Problem

Swiss firefighting knowledge is distributed across official documentation, manuals, guidelines, and role-related material. Users may need quick explanations of procedures, terminology, responsibilities, and Swiss firefighting practice, but manually searching through PDFs is slow and inconvenient.
